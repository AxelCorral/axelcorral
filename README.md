### Projects

**[football-pipeline](https://github.com/AxelCorral/football-pipeline)** — Ingestion
→ warehouse → models, in production. Raw/curated separation on S3 in Parquet, Athena
querying, per-competition models behind a CLI orchestrator. Five-stage GitLab CI with
a blocking 70% coverage gate. Results published per league, the unfavourable ones
included.

**[retraites-cor2026](https://github.com/AxelCorral/retraites-cor2026)** — Aggregate
accounting model of French pension sustainability on public COR data. Assumptions
stated up front, and a ~20-page report on what the projections do and do not support.

**[jobtrackr](https://github.com/AxelCorral/jobtrackr)** — Two official APIs with
incompatible formats in, no duplicates out. Idempotent deduplication, LLM scoring
decoupled from ingestion. Next.js, PostgreSQL, Vercel.

**[ombrair](https://github.com/AxelCorral/ombrair)** — Connected shutters that close
before a room overheats. Fictional product, university project: 24-hour thermal
simulator, 3D scenes, physics kept out of the interface in plain TypeScript modules
under unit test.

**[political-destiny](https://github.com/AxelCorral/political-destiny)** — Probabilistic
campaign simulator, audited across 1,890 simulated runs and 1,296 matched A/B pairs.
